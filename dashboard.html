Connect Pinterest Account

This tool runs locally on your computer. Your token never leaves your machine — it talks directly to Pinterest's servers via the backend (server.py).

⚠️ Important: Make sure server.py is running in your terminal before clicking Connect. You should see "Server running at http://localhost:5000" in your terminal.

How to get your Access Token:
1. Go to developers.pinterest.com/apps
2. Open your App → click Generate access token
3. Select scopes: boards:read, pins:read, user_accounts:read
4. Copy the token that starts with pina_
